### Requirements (18)

#### `::HashRelay.Access.TenantAdministration.TenantKeyIsolation` — Requirement

> An API credential resolves to exactly one tenant and cannot access another tenant's data.

#### `::HashRelay.Approvals.HumanDecision.DecisionIntegrity` — Requirement

> A decision is tenant-isolated, authorized against the designated approvers, committed at most once from Pending, and durably retrievable by the requester.

#### `::HashRelay.Approvals.HumanDecision.DeviceDecisionAuthority` — Requirement

> A device may submit DecideApproval only together with an active decision grant that the person it names gave explicitly, after interactive sign-in, to that paired device's credential in that workspace, and only that person may grant it. The pairing token alone never decides, and a device decides only from the approval's own notification as delivered to that device. At decision time the grant must be unrevoked and unexpired, its device credential still active, the approval in the grant's workspace, and the person still a member and a designated approver. Grants are revocable by the person and by the device.

#### `::HashRelay.Approvals.HumanDecision.OutcomeLifecycle` — Requirement

> An approval is created Pending and leaves Pending exactly once, to Approved or Rejected by a designated human's decision made while the server time is before expiresAt, to Expired when that time is reached, or to Cancelled by its requester. Terminal outcomes are immutable. The resolved event names its cause and names a decider only when a human decided.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.DesktopEndpoint.AgentDiagnostics` — Requirement

> Every agent reports its version, platform, endpoint id, connection state, last successful registration, notification-permission state and start-at-login state, and can export a diagnostic bundle of its recent logs. Diagnostics never contain the device credential or a decision grant.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.DesktopEndpoint.DesktopProtocolParity` — Requirement

> Every desktop agent, on every supported platform, pairs through the device-authorization flow, re-registers its endpoint after every connect and reconnect reporting its platform and agent version, acknowledges each delivery once received, suppresses notification ids it has recently presented, presents a replayed backlog of two or more as one catch-up summary (a lone replayed item, and a bounded number of high-priority items, may be shown on their own) while presenting live deliveries at once, and offers approval actions under the same rules. Platforms differ only in how they present, start, update and keep local secrets.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.DesktopEndpoint.LocalCredentialCustody` — Requirement

> The paired device credential and any decision grant an agent holds are kept in the signed-in user's platform secret store, readable only by that user, and never written to logs or diagnostics. Where no secret store is available the device credential is kept in a file readable only by that user, the agent reports this degraded custody in its diagnostics, it accepts no decision grant, and its approval actions stay review-intent only.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.DesktopEndpoint.NativeActionFallback` — Requirement

> An approval notification is presented on every platform. Where the platform cannot show actions on it, or the agent holds no active decision grant, its only action opens the first-party review page on the paired server's origin. An action on a terminal, expired or foreign-origin approval never decides.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.DesktopEndpoint.PerUserLifecycle` — Requirement

> Install, start-at-login, upgrade and uninstall are per user and need no administrator rights. Upgrade preserves the endpoint identity and pairing. Uninstall stops the agent, removes start-at-login and the notification registration, revokes any decision grant it holds and removes the local credential.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.DesktopEndpoint.UserSessionPresentation` — Requirement

> Notifications are presented only by the agent running in the signed-in user's session, through that platform's native notification facility, under a stable application identity. No system service or daemon outside the user session presents notifications. When the person has denied notification permission the agent says so in its own status rather than failing silently.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.DesktopEndpoint.VerifiedUpdates` — Requirement

> An agent installs only a release whose publisher signature verifies against the HashRelay publisher identity for its platform, obtained from the HashRelay release channel. An update needs no administrator rights, preserves the endpoint identity, and when it fails the installed version keeps running.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.HashtagDelivery.BoundedOfflineRetention` — Requirement

> A delivery queued for an offline endpoint is replayable for a bounded replay window set per deployment. When the window elapses the delivery becomes expired, whether or not the endpoint ever reconnects. A delivery handed to a connection but never acknowledged is retried on reconnect a bounded number of times. Expired is a terminal outcome that is reported as such and is never counted as delivered.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.HashtagDelivery.CatchUpWithoutInterruption` — Requirement

> Deliveries that accumulate while an endpoint is away are replayed on reconnect, flagged as replayed and retained until acknowledged or until their replay window elapses, so the endpoint can record them without presenting each one; live deliveries are presented at once. Acknowledgement means received, not shown.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.HashtagDelivery.DeliveryEvidence` — Requirement

> Provider acceptance of a push is recorded as provider acceptance and never reported as display or user acknowledgement; display is known only when the endpoint acknowledges the delivery.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.HashtagDelivery.ForwardOnlySubscription` — Requirement

> A subscription applies to notifications published after it exists. Earlier notifications are never replayed into a new subscription; they remain readable through the workspace's history.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.HashtagDelivery.OncePerEndpoint` — Requirement

> A notification matching multiple tags is logically delivered at most once per endpoint, with idempotent processing.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.HashtagDelivery.PublisherAttribution` — Requirement

> Every notification records who published it: a stable publisher id, a display name, a publisher type (agent, application, service, human or unknown) and the entry channel. All four are established by the routing service from the authenticated credential and its registration; none is taken from the message body or inferred from the transport. A type that was never declared is unknown.

#### `::HashRelay.Notifications.HashtagDelivery.WorkspaceIsolation` — Requirement

> Subscriptions, publishers and notifications are isolated by workspace.
